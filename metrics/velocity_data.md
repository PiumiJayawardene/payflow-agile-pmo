## Burndown Interpretation

The burndown pattern shows steady progress across all three sprints. Sprint 1 had a small carry-forward due to unclear mobile acceptance criteria, but Sprint 2 and Sprint 3 completed all committed work.

Sprint 3 velocity was lower than Sprint 1 and Sprint 2 by design. The sprint included launch-readiness activities such as load testing, penetration testing, smoke testing, and UAT preparation, which required coordination and quality focus rather than maximum story point output.

---

## Delivery Insights

1. The team maintained an average velocity above target.
2. Sprint 1 revealed the need for clearer UI acceptance criteria.
3. Sprint 2 showed that planned leave can be managed through capacity adjustment.
4. Sprint 3 confirmed that launch-readiness work should be planned with buffer.
5. No P1 or P2 defects remained open at the end of any sprint.
6. Delivery confidence increased across the project as velocity stabilised and team health improved.

---

## Scrum Master Notes

The PayFlow delivery team demonstrated strong Agile predictability across the three sprints. The average velocity of 36.7 points supports the original delivery plan and provides evidence of stable team performance.

The slight velocity decrease across sprints was expected because Sprint 3 prioritised quality, security, performance, and go-live readiness over additional feature volume.

This velocity data can be used in the GitHub README, project presentation, and portfolio screenshots to demonstrate Agile delivery tracking and PMO reporting capability.
