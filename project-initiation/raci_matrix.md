# RACI Matrix
## PayFlow Open Banking Payments Feature

**R** = Responsible — does the work  
**A** = Accountable — owns the outcome/sign-off  
**C** = Consulted — gives input before decisions  
**I** = Informed — kept updated  

| Activity | CEO / Sponsor | Product Owner | Scrum Master / PM | Engineering Lead | Dev Team | QA Engineer | Legal / Compliance |
|----------|----------------|---------------|-------------------|------------------|----------|-------------|--------------------|
| Project charter approval | A | C | R | C | I | I | I |
| Product backlog creation | I | A | C | C | C | I | I |
| Sprint planning | I | C | A/R | C | R | R | I |
| Daily standup facilitation | I | I | A/R | C | R | R | I |
| Architecture decisions | I | C | I | A/R | R | I | C |
| Open Banking API integration | I | C | I | A | R | C | C |
| FCA PSD2 compliance review | C | I | C | I | I | I | A/R |
| Sprint review facilitation | I | A | R | C | R | R | I |
| Sprint retrospective | I | I | A/R | C | R | R | I |
| User acceptance testing | C | A | R | C | I | R | C |
| Risk escalation | A | C | R | C | I | I | C |
| Go / No-Go decision | A | C | R | C | I | C | C |
| Go-live authorisation | A | C | R | C | I | C | C |
| Stakeholder status reporting | I | I | A/R | I | I | I | I |
| Lessons learned documentation | I | C | A/R | C | R | R | I |

---

## Key Governance Notes

- The CEO / Sponsor is accountable for major business decisions, budget approval, and final go-live authorisation.
- The Product Owner is accountable for backlog priority, acceptance of delivered features, and user value.
- The Scrum Master / PM is accountable for delivery coordination, ceremonies, reporting, risk tracking, and escalation.
- The Engineering Lead is accountable for technical decisions and implementation quality.
- Legal / Compliance is accountable for FCA PSD2 and Strong Customer Authentication compliance review.
