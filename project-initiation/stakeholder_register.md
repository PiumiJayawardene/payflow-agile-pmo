## Engagement Notes

- CEO should receive concise, outcome-focused updates rather than detailed sprint-level task information.
- Product Owner should be involved closely in backlog priority, scope decisions, and acceptance of sprint outcomes.
- Legal / Compliance should be engaged early to avoid late FCA PSD2 sign-off delays.
- Delivery team should receive clear sprint goals, well-defined stories, and fast blocker resolution.
- Pilot clients should be contacted before launch to validate real-world usability.
